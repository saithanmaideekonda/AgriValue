About AgriValue

Simple estimates to support a farmer’s decision

AgriValue uses historical agricultural market data and machine learning to estimate prices and compare potential revenue and net returns across markets.

A

Smarter Decisions for a More Profitable Harvest

Select any commodity available in the dataset, choose a state, district, and one or more markets, then enter quantity and your transportation-cost estimate. AgriValue calculates expected revenue, estimated net return, and historical market summaries to support your decision.

How to read the result

What AgriValue can and cannot tell you

What it does

  • Uses a Linear Regression model trained across the commodities in the current workbook.
  • The model estimates the commodity's Modal_Price in the recorded Price_Unit.
  • Calculates expected revenue using a quantity unit supported by the recorded price unit.
  • Subtracts your user-entered transportation-cost estimate.
  • Shows observed price variability, region, variety, grade, and quantity-range analysis.
  • Exports the current analysis as CSV, Excel, or PDF.

Important limits

  • Historical prices do not guarantee future prices.
  • The current workbook has limited temporal coverage and is not suitable for reliable long-term forecasting.
  • Price units are taken from the workbook. Unsupported conversions are not performed.
  • Transportation costs are user-entered fixed estimates and may differ from actual costs.
  • Model predictions depend on available historical records and may vary by commodity and location.
  • AgriValue provides decision-support estimates and does not guarantee profit.
